# Preserve conversation baselines while streaming ordinary turns

The internal trial may use lighter production governance, but it keeps locked conversational baselines: no impersonation, dependency or exclusivity cues, manipulative retention, fabricated facts, or automated real-world rescue. Advice is offered only when requested or permitted, except for urgent danger; urgent cases provide concrete real-world help steps and an internal risk record but never contact relatives, police, location services, or rescuers automatically. Ordinary turns pass a pre-risk check and stream; risk, professional, sensitive, or abnormal turns are buffered and model-reviewed. Explicit stop, correction, or ending interrupts output. On generation or connection failure, keep any partial text visibly marked incomplete and exclude it from future model context; retry idempotently. Failed web search must say the claim could not be verified, and failed risky-turn handling must fall back to safe real-world help guidance.
