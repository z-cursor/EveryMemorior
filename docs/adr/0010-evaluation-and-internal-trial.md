# Reuse model-scored companionship evaluations for the internal trial

The administrator-only evaluation UI reuses the emotion-companionship evaluation method instead of maintaining a second fixed regression bank. A draft quick run evaluates either eight randomly sampled single-turn cases or one complete five-turn episode, returning dimension scores, fatal issues, and change suggestions; a human decides whether to edit, rerun, publish, or reject. The full 500-case suite is a stage gate for expanding the trial. The FY model may generate and grade only in isolated contexts, with grading at temperature zero, no thinking, and structured output. Before score deltas guide decisions, at least twelve deliberately high, low, and borderline cases receive human calibration. Each result snapshots config, questions, private answers, grader prompt, grader model, and parameters; scores are directly comparable only within one grader version, with bridge re-evaluation after grader changes. Trial 10–20 older adults for at least two weeks, beginning with scripted tasks and then free chat; permit ordinary life discussion, prohibit direct identifiers, and use fictional assigned cases for medical, financial, trauma, and risk scenarios. Success requires zero baseline violations, at least 80% specifically responsive sampled conversations without interrogation or ignored endings, under 2% memory/personal-fact errors, latency targets, and at least 70% of completed exit interviews expressing willingness to continue, always reported with numerator, denominator, and missing interviews.
